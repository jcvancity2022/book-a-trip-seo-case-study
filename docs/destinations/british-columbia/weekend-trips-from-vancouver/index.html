6 best weekend trips from Vancouver

Vancouver sits within a couple of hours of mountain resort towns, hot springs, islands, and an adventure hub, most reachable by a short drive or ferry. Here's what's actually at each one, ranked by how well it fills a full weekend, and how to get there.

  1. 1Whistler
  2. 2Squamish
  3. 3Harrison Hot Springs
  4. 4Bowen Island
  5. 5Sunshine Coast
  6. 6Vancouver Island

1. Whistler

~1.5 hour drive via the Sea-to-Sky Highway

A four-season resort town with the Peak 2 Peak Gondola, lake swimming in summer, mountain bike trails, and a walkable village. Works well as a day trip, but a full weekend leaves room to actually get up the mountain rather than just stroll the village.

2. Squamish

~40–45 minute drive

Known as the Adventure Capital of Canada — hiking, rock climbing, mountain biking, and kiteboarding. The Sea to Sky Gondola climbs 2,900 feet for views of the Chief and Howe Sound, with a suspension bridge and trails to Shannon Falls at the base. Transit options here are limited, so a car is recommended.

3. Harrison Hot Springs

A small town at the southern end of Harrison Lake, built around natural mineral hot springs, a lagoon, and mountain views — a slower-paced, relaxation-focused option compared to the adventure towns above.

4. Bowen Island

~20 minute ferry from Horseshoe Bay

Close enough for a half-day, far enough to feel like a real break from the city — wooded trails, boardwalks, and small shops near the ferry terminal.

5. Sunshine Coast (Sechelt)

Short ferry ride from Horseshoe Bay

Hiking, mountain biking, and kayaking along the coast north of Vancouver — a good pick if the goal is outdoor time without driving all the way to the island.

6. Vancouver Island (Victoria)

~1 hour 40 minutes by ferry

BC's capital, built around the Inner Harbour and Butchart Gardens (55 acres, over 900 plant varieties). Reachable by ferry, bus, or float plane from Vancouver.
