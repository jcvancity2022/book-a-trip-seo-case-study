Vancouver to Victoria ferry: routes, times, and cost

One ferry operator runs this crossing -- BC Ferries, between Tsawwassen and Swartz Bay. The real decision is how you get to and from the terminals, and whether that's worth paying the Connector bus to handle for you.

The route

BC Ferries sails between Tsawwassen (about 45 minutes south of downtown Vancouver) and Swartz Bay (about 30 minutes north of downtown Victoria), carrying both vehicles and foot passengers. It's the only direct ferry route between the two cities -- there's no equivalent to the Hullo/BC Ferries choice on the Nanaimo crossing here.

What does vary is how you reach the terminal on each end. Most guides stop at the sailing itself; the gap worth filling is what happens before and after it.

How long does it take?

  • The sailing itself: about 1 hour 35 minutes to 2 hours, Tsawwassen–Swartz Bay
  • Door-to-door, downtown Vancouver to downtown Victoria: roughly 3 to 3.5 hours once you count getting to Tsawwassen, boarding, and the drive/bus from Swartz Bay into Victoria

How much does it cost?

Fares checked 22 Sep 2026 — confirm current pricing before booking, these change often and by sailing.

Foot passengers: BC Ferries' Saver Fare is a flat $15 CAD one-way adult ($7.50 child), on this route as on the other major Vancouver Island crossings -- booked online in advance, paid in full at booking, not available at the terminal.

Vehicle and driver: Saver Fares run $49–99 CAD one-way depending on the sailing, with a higher prepaid/at-terminal rate outside Saver availability.

Regular (non-Saver, walk-up) fares are higher and shift by day of week and season -- there isn't one honest single number to quote here. Use BC Ferries' fare calculator for your actual travel date rather than trusting a page like this one for the exact figure.

Getting to Tsawwassen terminal without a car

This is the part most guides for this route leave thin. The actual route: take the Canada Line SkyTrain to Bridgeport Station, then TransLink's 620 bus (Tsawwassen Ferry / Bridgeport Station Express), which runs roughly hourly from about 6am to 10pm daily, departing Bridgeport and arriving directly at the Tsawwassen terminal. Confirm the current schedule on TransLink's own route page before you go -- frequency can change.

On the Victoria side, Swartz Bay connects into downtown Victoria via BC Transit buses, or the Connector coach if you booked that ticket.

Prefer one booking over cheapest: the BC Ferries Connector folds the SkyTrain-and-bus routing above into a single door-to-door ticket -- worth it if you'd rather not manage two transfers each way, even though doing it yourself on transit plus the $15 Saver fare is the cheaper path.

Frequently asked questions

How long is the ferry from Vancouver to Victoria?

About 1 hour 35 minutes to 2 hours for the crossing itself, or roughly 3 to 3.5 hours door-to-door between downtown Vancouver and downtown Victoria.

How much does the ferry cost from Vancouver to Victoria?

Foot-passenger Saver fares are a flat $15 CAD one-way; a vehicle and driver runs $49–99 CAD one-way on Saver Fares. Regular walk-up fares are higher and vary -- check BC Ferries' fare calculator for your date.

How do I get to Tsawwassen ferry terminal without a car?

Canada Line SkyTrain to Bridgeport Station, then TransLink's 620 bus, roughly hourly between about 6am and 10pm. The BC Ferries Connector is the one-ticket alternative if you'd rather not manage the transfer yourself.

Is there a direct bus from Vancouver to Victoria that includes the ferry?

Yes -- the BC Ferries Connector: one ticket for a coach pickup in Vancouver plus the ferry crossing, dropping you in downtown Victoria. Costs more than doing it yourself on transit, but it's a single booking with no transfers.
