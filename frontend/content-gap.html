Mirrors Ahrefs' Content Gap tool from the on-page SEO lesson. Enter 2–3 real competitor URLs for a page, run them through the tool yourself, and log what it actually shows here — nothing on this page generates a number on its own. See also: the keyword research log and the link prospect log.

The method, exactly as taught: feed in the 2–3 competitor URLs you selected for a page. A keyword is worth adding to your content plan if it shows up under the "2 targets" or "3 targets" intersection — meaning at least 2 of your competitors rank for it, and at least one of them ranks in the top 10. A keyword only 1 target ranks for is weaker evidence and generally not worth chasing on its own.
