BC Ferries discounts: who actually qualifies, and for what

Several different programs, several similar-sounding names -- senior fares, a disability discount, and two entirely separate "assured loading" programs that aren't discounts at all. Here's each one, verified directly, without the parts that get lost when only one program is covered at a time.

Senior fare

Free, Monday to Thursday, for BC residents 65 and older, on most routes -- with a valid BC Services Card, BC driver's licence, or BC ID. On the North Coast, Central Coast, and Haida Gwaii routes, it's a reduced fare, not free, every day instead. Seniors who aren't BC residents get no discount at all and book the regular adult fare.

That three-way distinction -- free vs. reduced vs. nothing, depending on residency and route -- is exactly what tends to get flattened into a blanket "seniors ride free" claim elsewhere.

Disability fare

BC residents with a permanent disability get 50% off the passenger fare, plus 50% off for one personal attendant. It requires a BC Ferries Accessible Fare Identification (AFI) card or a CNIB card, presented to the ticket agent.

Three "assured loading" programs, disambiguated

These share overlapping names but are genuinely different products -- only one is a fare discount.

The mix-up to watch for: "assured loading" on its own usually means the general card, not the disability or medical programs -- check which one a source is actually talking about before assuming it applies to you.

Student (youth) fare

BC students 18 and under travel free with valid ID -- but only for trips between home and school, or to school and junior-association sporting or education events. It's not a general youth discount for any trip.

BC Experience Card

A prepaid fare card (minimum $100 top-up) that discounts fares on select minor, ferry-dependent routes. On the Nanaimo and Victoria routes this site covers, BC Ferries' own terms state Experience Card savings don't apply -- it works only as a payment method there, not a discount. See BC Ferries' Experience Card page for routes where it does discount fares.

Frequently asked questions

Do seniors travel free on BC Ferries?

Only BC residents 65+, only Monday to Thursday, on most routes. Reduced (not free) every day on North Coast, Central Coast, and Haida Gwaii routes. Non-BC-resident seniors get no discount.

What is the BC Ferries disability discount?

50% off the passenger fare, plus 50% off for one attendant, with an AFI or CNIB card.

What's the difference between Assured Loading and Medical Assured Loading?

The Assured Loading Card guarantees boarding for anyone arriving 20+ minutes early -- not a discount. Medical Assured Loading is TAP-patients-only with a doctor's letter, also not a discount. Neither is the AFI disability discount.

Does the BC Experience Card work on the Nanaimo or Victoria ferry?

Usable as payment on both, but no savings apply there -- discounts are limited to select minor routes.

Is there a youth discount on BC Ferries?

Free for BC students 18 and under, but only for home-to-school travel or school/junior-association events -- not a general youth fare.

Verified 24 Sep 2026 against BC Ferries' own pages -- fare and eligibility rules change, confirm current terms at bcferries.com before relying on any of the above.
