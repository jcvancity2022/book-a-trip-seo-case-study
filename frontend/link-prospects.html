Link prospect log

Mirrors the Ahrefs link-building module's five-attribute scorecard (relevance, authority, anchor text, follow status, placement) and its prospecting → vetting → outreach process. Rows are only ever added from a page someone actually opened and read — the authority field stays "Needs Ahrefs" until a live Ahrefs session checks DR/UR. See also: the keyword research log and the content gap log.

The scorecard, applied here: relevance and a real editorial spot come first — a high-authority page with nowhere honest to place the link is still a skip. Authority (Domain Rating / URL Rating) is a tiebreaker between relevant options, not the entry test, and is left blank until it's actually checked in Ahrefs. links_out records whether the site hyperlinks to anything external at all — a relevant page that never links out is also a skip, regardless of authority.
